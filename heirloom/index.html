H-sib Heirloom

Privacy policy. Version 1.0. Last updated 5 November 2026.

Who we are

H-sib Heirloom is a private family tree app made by H-sib (Chowdhury Hasibur Rahaman). Contact: the email shown on the Google Play page of the app.

Short version

The app collects nothing. Your family stays on your phone. The app has no account and no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

What the app stores, on your phone only

  • The people you add, how they are related, and anything you type about them: names, dates, places, stories.
  • Photos and documents you add to a person. They are copied into the app on your device.
  • Your settings, such as the theme, sounds and the words used for relatives.

All of this stays inside the app on your device. Removing the app removes it.

Files you save, print or share

When you save a backup, a picture or a PDF, you choose where it goes using your phone's own save screen. When you import a file, you choose it with your phone's own file picker. The app never uploads these files. Once saved, they are yours to keep, move or delete.

Permissions

The app asks only for permission to vibrate the phone, for touch feedback. It has no internet permission. It does not ask for your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or storage.

Advertising and tracking

None. No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no advertising ID.

Children

The app is for ages 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings, then Archive, then Erase the archive. You can also uninstall the app, or clear its data in your phone's settings. Backup files you saved yourself are separate: delete them in your files app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text will be on this page with a new date. A future version that adds sign-in or sharing between relatives will update this policy first.
